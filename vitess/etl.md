# PlanetScale Connect

> PlanetScale Connect is one of the underlying mechanisms we use to transfer data between parties.

This page covers Vitess.

Postgres version: [Postgres](https://planetscale.com/docs/postgres/integrations/logical-cdc.md)

With PlanetScale Connect, you can integrate with supported ETL platforms to extract data from your PlanetScale database and safely load it into other destinations for analysis, transformation, and more.

## ETL integrations

We currently support the following connectors:

> [card] [Airbyte](https://planetscale.com/docs/vitess/integrations/airbyte.md)

> [card] [Debezium](https://planetscale.com/docs/vitess/integrations/debezium.md)

> [card] [Fivetran](https://planetscale.com/docs/vitess/integrations/fivetran.md)

> [card] [Hightouch](https://planetscale.com/docs/vitess/integrations/hightouch.md)
