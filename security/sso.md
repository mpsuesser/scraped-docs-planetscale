# Single sign-on

> Single sign-on (SSO) provides additional account security by allowing company administrators to require the use of an identity provider when logging into PlanetScale.

## Overview

Users only need to sign in once with a single set of credentials *(i.e. password and email)* to access all of their tools and applications upon joining the company.

Furthermore, SSO allows an administrator to revoke someone’s access to all tools and applications from a single place when they leave a team or the company. PlanetScale uses SAML SSO.

You can also enable and manage organization SSO from the [PlanetScale CLI](../cli/org.md).

> [!NOTE]
>
> SSO is available as an *add-on* for the [Base plan](../planetscale-plans.md#base-plan) at \$199/month per organization and included in our [Enterprise plans](../planetscale-plans.md#enterprise-plan). Security is important to us, so we do not profit off of SSO. We only charge enough to cover the WorkOS cost for enrolling a new account.

## Implications

It's important to understand how enabling SSO will affect your Organization. Once enabled, the following will happen:

* All non-admin members will be removed from the organization.
* [Organization Administrators](access-control.md#organization-administrator) will remain in the Organization so they can configure SSO without losing access.
  * All administrators will retain access with their old credentials, until they logout and login through their Identity Provider. Once they've authenticated through their Identity Provider the account will only be usable with SSO authentication.
* Each [Organization Member](https://app.planetscale.com/planetscale/settings/members) must re-authenticate using SSO. Once they've authenticated, they will be automatically added back to the organization.
* Organization Member invites will be disabled when SSO is enabled; all Organization Membership will be managed through SSO.
* Organization Members that were [Database Administrators](access-control.md#database-administrator) before will no longer have that role upon rejoining. You must assign them the role after they re-authenticate with SSO.
* Organization Members that were on [Teams](teams.md) will need to be re-added.
* Any database credentials and tokens that were generated by non-admin members will remain active, so you **do not need to regenerate connection credentials**.
* Organization Members removed from your SSO will still appear in the Organization until they are manually removed.
  * While they are visible in the Organization they will not be able to authenticate to PlanetScale.
  * If you enabled [Directory Sync](#directory-sync), the Member will be removed from the Organization without manual intervention.
* Organization Members can only authenticate through SSO when their email address uses a [verified domain](#domain-verification).

> [!NOTE]
>
> If you enable SSO and Directory Sync, the Directory will remain the source of truth, and Teams will map accordingly. Please see the [Directory Sync](#directory-sync) section for more information.

## Domain verification

Verify ownership of each email domain your organization uses for SSO. Users can sign in through SSO only when their email address uses a verified domain. You can start verification from the dashboard or with `pscale org sso domain verify`.

> [!NOTE]
>
> The **Configure single sign-on** button stays disabled until at least one domain is verified.

### Verify a domain

**Step 1**

Go to your [PlanetScale organization's authentication page](https://app.planetscale.com/~/settings/authentication) under **Settings** → **Authentication**.

**Step 2**

Under the **Single sign-on** section, click the **Verify domain** button.

**Step 3**

You will be redirected to the WorkOS domain verification portal. Follow the steps there to verify your domain by adding a DNS TXT record to your domain's DNS configuration.

**Step 4**

Once WorkOS confirms the DNS record, your domain will appear as **Verified** on the authentication page.

DNS propagation can take a few minutes to a few hours depending on your DNS provider and TTL settings. When at least one domain shows **Verified**, continue with [Enable SSO for your organization](#enable-sso-for-your-organization).

### Manage verified domains

Verified and pending domains are listed on the authentication page. You can delete a domain at any time using the **Delete** button.

Verify every domain your members use to sign in (for example, `company.com` and `subsidiary.com`). If SSO is enabled and a member's email uses a domain you have not verified, they cannot authenticate through SSO.

> [!WARNING]
>
> If you delete a verified domain, users with email addresses on that domain can no longer authenticate through SSO. If SSO is already enabled, keep at least one verified domain for every email domain your members use.

## Enable SSO for your organization

To enable SSO for your organization, you must be an [Organization Administrator](access-control.md). Organization administrators can *enable*, *configure*, and *disable* SSO for all members of your organization in the dashboard or with [`pscale org sso`](../cli/org.md).

**Step 1**

[Verify your organization's domain(s)](#domain-verification).

**Step 2**

Head to your [PlanetScale organization's authentication page](https://app.planetscale.com/~/settings/authentication) under **Settings** → **Authentication** and click **Enable single sign-on**.

**Step 3**

Once SSO is enabled, click **Configure single sign-on**. This will take you to WorkOS where you can choose your identity provider. You can find documentation for your specific identity provider in the [WorkOS integrations documentation](https://workos.com/integrations).

You also have the option to manage PlanetScale roles through your identity provider's SSO profile. Just check the box next to that message on the settings page to enable.

> [!NOTE]
>
> If you are using Okta as your identity provider and want to manage organization roles through SSO or Directory Sync, see [Using Okta to manage organization admins](#using-okta-to-manage-organization-admins) for additional configuration steps.

## Multiple organizations with SSO

You can enable SSO for multiple organizations using the same identity provider. Users will be able to swap between organizations by using the selector in the top right of the PlanetScale dashboard.

> [!TIP]
>
> When setting up SSO for multiple organizations, make sure to check that the role management settings are configured correctly for each organization. The settings for each organization are independent of each other.

## Disable SSO

When SSO is disabled for an organization, users can log in with the password they initially set for their PlanetScale account. If they don't know their password, users can go through the password reset flow to regain access to their account.

Should a user lose access to the email address associated with that organization, they'll also lose access to their account after SSO is disabled.

## Directory Sync

We also support the use of Directory Sync with SSO. You can use Directory Sync to make the directory the source of truth for organization membership. You can open the directory setup portal with `pscale org sso directory enable`.

### Enable Directory Sync

To enable Directory Sync, you first must have SSO enabled.

Once enabled, go to your Organization settings page, click "**Authentication**", and click the "**Enable directory sync**" button.

You can now configure Directory Sync using your identity provider.

### Directory Sync access control

Directory Sync automatically adds and removes members from your PlanetScale organization to match your SSO directory. If you have groups defined within your SSO provider, it can also automatically create [Teams](teams.md) within your PlanetScale organization mapped to those groups.

If you wish to have your identity provider determine user roles in PlanetScale, please make sure to select the option for `Manage PlanetScale roles through identity provider` in Settings > Authentication.

![Manage roles](https://mintcdn.com/planetscale-2/GA0k5H-MolPvBjDk/images/assets/docs/concepts/sso/sync.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=GA0k5H-MolPvBjDk\&q=85\&s=55861fac34c727f7a2805771c82cda7d)

> [!NOTE]
>
> Once you enable Directory Sync, existing Teams will be cleared, as all Teams must map to a Directory group.

You can find the directory-managed members under "**Settings**" > "**Members**", and directory-managed Teams under "**Settings**" > "**Teams**".

## Admin failsafe

When PlanetScale roles are managed through SSO or Directory Sync, PlanetScale keeps at least one `admin` in the organization. This failsafe helps prevent identity provider or directory role mapping misconfiguration from removing every admin, so an admin can update settings and fix the configuration.

## Using Okta to manage organization admins

There are two different ways to configure Okta to manage the user's role in a PlanetScale organization. The first approach uses SAML assertions during Single Sign On to update the role just-in-time. The second approach uses an attribute defined on the directory user to update the role in real time as it changes. Choose the approach that works best for your organization.

### Managing roles during single sign-on (SAML)

In order to update roles using SAML, you will first need to configure the SAML application and user profile in Okta before enabling the setting within PlanetScale.

#### Configuring the SAML Okta application

**Step 1**

Click the **Applications** > **Applications** in the Okta dashboard's sidebar.

**Step 2**

Find your PlanetScale SAML application that you configured earlier for WorkOS and find the **SAML Settings** section. Click on the **Edit** link in this section.

**Step 3**

In the **Configure SAML** step, find the **Attribute Statements (optional)** section, which defines the attributes of email, first and last name, and id.

**Step 4**

Add a new Attribute Statement with the following values.

* Name — `planetscale_role`
* Name Format — `Unspecified`
* Value — `user.planetscale_role`

#### Creating user profile attributes in Okta

In order to define the custom attribute in Okta, you must update the default User profile to have the `planetscale_role` attribute.

**Step 1**

Click **Directory** > **Profile Editor** in the Okta dashboard's sidebar.

**Step 2**

Click the **User (default)** profile within Okta

**Step 3**

Click **Add attribute** and fill in the following values

* Data type: `string`
* Display name: `PlanetScale Role`
* Variable name: `planetscale_role`
* Enum: Enable the **Define enumerated list of values** checkbox and add these attributes:
  * Display name: `Member`, Value: `member`
  * Display name: `Analyst`, Value: `analyst`
  * Display name: `Administrator`, Value: `admin`

**Step 4**

Click **Save attribute**

#### Enable in PlanetScale

Lastly, we will have to enable role management via SSO within the PlanetScale application.

**Step 1**

Go to your SSO organization's settings page

**Step 2**

Click on **Authentication** in the side navigation

**Step 3**

Check the **Manage PlanetScale roles through identity provider's SSO profile** checkbox

After following all of these steps, after you change the PlanetScale role for a user in Okta, their role will be updated accordingly the next time they log in via Single Sign On. If the role is changed during an active session, they will need to log out and log back in for it to be updated once again.

### Managing roles using Directory Sync (SCIM)

In order to manage roles using Directory Sync, you must properly configure the SCIM user's profile within Okta and update the Directory Sync custom attributes before enabling the setting within the PlanetScale dashboard.

#### Configuring the SCIM user profile in Okta

**Step 1**

Click **Directory** > **Profile Editor** in the Okta dashboard's sidebar.

**Step 2**

Click the user profile that maps to your SCIM application, it should be in the format of **\< SCIM application name > User**

**Step 3**

Click **Add attribute** and fill in the following values

* Data type: `string`
* Display name: `PlanetScale Role`
* Variable name: `planetscale_role`
* External name: `planetscale_role`
* The External namespace should be defined as `urn:ietf:params:scim:schemas:core:2.0:User`.
* Check the box to define an enumerated list of values, add the following:
  * Display name: `member`, Value: `member`
  * Display name: `analyst`, Value: `analyst`
  * Display name: `administrator`, Value: `admin`

**Step 4**

Click **Save attribute**

![PlanetScale Role](https://mintcdn.com/planetscale-2/GA0k5H-MolPvBjDk/images/assets/docs/concepts/sso/planetscale_role.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=GA0k5H-MolPvBjDk\&q=85\&s=c22f15bf288b9222d6ee1f6e22202115)

This attribute then needs to be mapped to the PlanetScale application in Okta.

**Step 1**

Open the PlanetScale SCIM application in Okta Admin Console, then select Provisioning

**Step 2**

Scroll down to the bottom of the `Okta to App` provisioning page, and click `Show unmapped attributes`.

**Step 3**

Find `planetscale_role` and click the 🖋️ to map the attribute.

**Step 4**

Select `Map from Okta Profile` as the type and `planetscale_role` as the string.

**Step 5**

Save with Create & Update permission.

#### Configuring custom attributes within WorkOS

Next, you will need to update your WorkOS configuration to detect the custom attribute mappings.

**Step 1**

Go to the **Authentication** within your organization settings page.

**Step 2**

In the **Directory sync** section, click the `identity provider` link to open up the directory configuration page

**Step 3**

Click the **Edit Attribute Map** button and in the form, enter the following:

* Directory Provider Value: `planetscale_role`
* PlanetScale Attribute: `planetscale_role`

**Step 4**

Click **Save mappings**

#### Enable in PlanetScale

Lastly, we will have to enable role management via SSO within the PlanetScale application.

**Step 1**

Go to your SSO organization's settings page

**Step 2**

Click on **Authentication** in the side navigation

**Step 3**

Check the **Manage PlanetScale roles through identity provider's directory** checkbox

After following all of these steps, after you change the PlanetScale role for a user in Okta, their role will be updated in PlanetScale without needing to re-authenticate.
